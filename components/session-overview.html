Want to join in person in Carlingford?

Maybe you're wondering what it's like when you come along. Fair question! Here's a little snapshot of what you can expect.

Each week, a group of us gets together to explore what the Bible has to say about life's big questions. All sorts of people come along: believers and non-believers, young and old, people who grew up going to church and people who have never been before. Everyone is welcome.

Grab a drink and a snack, settle in, and join the conversation. We spend time listening, discussing and exploring together.

There's no cost, and each session goes for about an hour. So come along, grab a drink, and see what you think.
