Want to join us in person in Carlingford?

Exploring faith is better when you don't have to do it alone. We've created a relaxed space where anyone and everyone can meet, ask questions and explore together.

Wondering what it's like to come along? Here’s a little taste of what you can expect.

When you arrive, there’s plenty of free parking. Inside, you’ll find people already chatting over tea, coffee and snacks. We’ll kick off with some easy questions to help everyone get talking and get to know each other. Then there’s a short talk, followed by plenty of time for questions and discussion — no question is off limits.

Afterwards, you’re welcome to stick around for a chat, or simply head home. The whole thing takes a little over an hour.

All sorts of people come along — believers and non-believers, young and old, people who grew up around church and people who have never stepped inside one before. Everyone is welcome.

So grab a drink and a snack, settle in, and join the conversation. There’s no pressure and no cost — just space to listen, ask questions and explore together.
