Want to join us in person in Carlingford?

Exploring faith is better when you don't have to do it alone. We've created a relaxed space where anyone can come and learn about faith alongside other people.

When you arrive there’s plenty of free parking. Inside, you’ll find people already chatting over tea and snacks. Then there’s a short talk and interviews, with some easy group discussion, followed by plenty of time for questions — no question is off limits.

Afterwards, you’re welcome to stick around for a chat, or simply head home. The whole thing takes a little over an hour.

All sorts of people come along — believers and non-believers, young and old, people who grew up around church and people who have never stepped inside one before. Everyone is welcome.

There’s no pressure to believe anything and no cost to attend — just space to listen, ask questions and explore together.
